Thought After 2AM — transparent sprite reference crops

The source sprite sheet contains alpha transparency, preserved in these PNGs.
The crops are separated reference assets, not yet a fully aligned skeletal rig.
Before production rigging: manually inspect crop boundaries, split combined
rows into individual eye/mouth/hand sprites, align parts on a shared canvas,
and paint hidden overlap areas at shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips and knees.
No claim of automatic bone rigging or phoneme-level lip sync is made here.
